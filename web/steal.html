Steal a look

Upload a photo whose look you want — a post you saved, a film scan, a frame from a film. The pipeline separates its grade from its scene, so you get the look without the other photo's colors bleeding in, then applies it to a shot of yours.

    Why the slider starts halfway

    Guessing a grade from a single photo is genuinely ill-posed: the same picture could be a warm scene shot neutrally or a neutral scene graded warm. Measured against seven known grades, the best blind estimate beats doing nothing by about 12% — and only when applied at partial strength. Give it the before/after pair and that ambiguity disappears.
